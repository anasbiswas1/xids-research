## 8. Method notes recorded for the manuscript

- FGSM and PGD perturbations are generated from dnn_5class_cw and transferred to the other five models in each dataset.

- PGD projection is onto the L-infinity ball of radius epsilon (clamp to x plus or minus epsilon).

- c2 is the minimum over the three perturbations of the Jaccard index between clean and perturbed top-10 feature sets; the retention variant (|A and B| / k) is stored in the per-sample table as c2_retention.

- v2 protocol: Mondrian thresholds, cliff fractions and support counts are measured on test-outside-canonical. Strict protocol: on the 20% holdout of the calibration partition, with calibrators refit on the other 80%.
